Cross-Border Gateway

Colombo Associates International Desk

Your Sri Lankan point of contact for cross-border business and legal matters. Supporting overseas companies, foreign investors, international law firms and professional advisers with coordinated Sri Lankan legal and corporate support.

Bridging Jurisdictions

A responsive coordination bridge for overseas principals and global advisers.

Cross-border expansion, corporate investment and commercial disputes in Sri Lanka often involve local statutory requirements, regulatory considerations and administrative procedures.

The International Desk provides a single point of contact for initial issue assessment, Sri Lankan legal and corporate coordination, document support, regulatory mapping and communication with relevant advisers.

We support international clients through clear English-language communication, structured reporting and coordination within the firm's authorised scope under Sri Lankan law.

Desk Scope

Core Coordination Functions

  • 1. Initial Issue Assessment: Preliminary assessment of the matter, relevant jurisdiction, statutory considerations and procedural options in Sri Lanka.
  • 2. Legal & Corporate Coordination: Coordination of Sri Lankan legal advice, corporate requirements, documentation and statutory filings.
  • 3. Regulatory Mapping: Identifying relevant Sri Lankan regulatory requirements and potential approval processes, including applicable BOI, foreign exchange and Port City considerations where relevant.
  • 4. Adviser Collaboration: Coordination with international law firms, accounting practices, professional advisers and investment consultants.
* Colombo Associates is an independent legal practice in Sri Lanka and is not affiliated with government authorities. Advisory coordination does not guarantee regulatory approvals.

Who We Assist

  • • Overseas Companies: Multinationals establishing branches, project offices or subsidiaries in Sri Lanka.
  • • Foreign Investors & Funds: International founders, investors and funds seeking Sri Lankan legal and corporate support.
  • • International Law Firms: Overseas legal practices seeking reliable Sri Lankan local counsel coordination.
  • • Professional Advisers: Accountants, consultants and business advisers coordinating cross-border matters.
  • • Sri Lankan Businesses: Domestic enterprises with international commercial, investment or contractual requirements.

Typical Matters Handled

  • • Market Entry & Incorporation: Business establishment, entity formation, capital structuring and applicable registration requirements.
  • • Shareholder & Commercial Matters: Shareholder rights, joint ventures, commercial agreements and corporate arrangements.
  • • Due Diligence & Compliance: Corporate reviews, relevant document verification, statutory compliance and regulatory mapping.
  • • Contracts & Documentation: Commercial contracts, corporate documentation and supporting legal documentation.
  • • Sri Lankan Dispute Support: Coordination of local litigation, dispute resolution and institutional arbitration where within the firm's authorised scope.
  • • Ongoing Corporate Requirements: Continuing statutory filings, corporate governance and company secretarial coordination.
Note: Services remain subject to professional authorisation, conflict checks and formal engagement terms. Matters are not automatically accepted.
Structured Delivery

How It Works: The 4-Stage Client Journey

A transparent, reliable onboarding and execution framework designed for international confidence.

1

Stage 01 – Submit Initial Enquiry

The client or international counsel submits a brief outline of the requirements, relevant entities and anticipated timeline for preliminary assessment.

2

Stage 02 – Conflict & Scope Checks

The firm reviews potential conflicts of interest, verifies relevant information and assesses whether the matter falls within its authorised professional scope.

3

Stage 03 – Engagement Terms & Documents

Subject to acceptance, the parties confirm engagement terms, applicable fees, client identification requirements and necessary documentation.

4

Stage 04 – Agreed Work Plan & Updates

The firm establishes agreed milestones, communication arrangements, reporting expectations and appropriate coordination with relevant advisers.
