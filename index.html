Cross-Border Focus

Colombo Associates International Desk

Your Sri Lankan point of contact for cross-border business and legal matters. We coordinate Sri Lankan statutory, corporate, and procedural requirements for foreign principals and international legal advisers.

  • Who we assist: Overseas companies, foreign investors, international law firms, professional advisers, and Sri Lankan firms with cross-border operations.
  • How we assist: Initial issue assessment, Sri Lankan legal and corporate coordination, document legalisation support, regulatory mapping, and direct liaison with foreign counsel.
  • Typical matters: Market entry, BOI coordination, shareholder matters, commercial contracts, due diligence, and Sri Lankan dispute coordination.
