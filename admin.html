How to test the Database & Enquiry Submission:

  1. Open contact.html in a new browser tab.
  2. Fill out the form with sample information (Name, Email, Phone, Country, Service Area, Matter Description) and check the consent box.
  3. Click Submit Enquiry. You will see the green success card with a unique Reference ID (e.g. CA-ENQ-2026-XXXX).
  4. Come back to this Admin tab and click Refresh Data (or refresh page). Your new submission will immediately appear in the table above!
  5. You can also inspect the raw JSON database anytime directly at data/enquiries/enquiries.json.
