Approved Positioning

Authorised Sri Lankan legal and corporate services for local enterprises, foreign investors, and international advisers.

Colombo Associates delivers multidisciplinary legal, corporate and strategic support to domestic enterprises, foreign investors, institutions and professional advisers navigating matters in Sri Lanka and across borders.

Rooted in commercial realities and guided by an international outlook, our practice brings together legal advisory, corporate governance, and statutory compliance into a coordinated, responsive client journey. We provide clear assessments, defined next steps, and disciplined follow-through strictly within our authorised scope of Sri Lankan practice.

Our service model is defined by practical guidance, straightforward communication free from unnecessary legalese, and an unwavering commitment to professionalism, integrity, and responsiveness.

Professional Standards & Scope

Clarity of Scope & Regulatory Compliance

In strict compliance with professional guidelines, Colombo Associates clearly distinguishes legal advisory and court representation provided by admitted Attorneys-at-Law from company secretarial administrations and compliance services delivered through qualified Corporate Secretaries. We do not purport to advise on foreign laws and maintain complete conflict clearance protocols before accepting matters.
