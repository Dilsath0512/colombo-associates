Practice Areas & Capabilities

Explore our legal, corporate, regulatory and advisory practice areas, covering commercial matters, disputes, governance and specialised services in Sri Lanka.

Professional Scope & Regulatory Notice: Practice categories reflect standard legal and commercial taxonomy. Colombo Associates strictly distinguishes legal advisory and courtroom representation (conducted exclusively by enrolled Attorneys-at-Law of the Supreme Court of Sri Lanka under the Judicature Act) from corporate secretarial administration (managed by qualified Company Secretaries registered under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007) and notarial attestations (executed by licensed Notaries Public appointed under the Notaries Ordinance). Colombo Associates does not practice or advise on foreign law; cross-border matters are coordinated alongside qualified counsel in relevant foreign jurisdictions. Inclusion of a practice category reflects firm capabilities and does not constitute formal legal advice or create an attorney-client relationship. All formal instructions require preliminary conflict clearance, verification of authorised scope, and formal execution of written engagement terms.
